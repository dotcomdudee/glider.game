# Glider — One throw. Endless sky.

**Free to play · 29 destinations · 13 gliders · Controller support · Cloud saves**

**[Fly now at glider.game →](https://glider.game/)**

A folded sheet of paper. A little momentum. An entire world beneath your wings.

Glider is an atmospheric 3D paper-plane game about the simple pleasure of flight. Skim turquoise lakes, carve through golden canyons, drift beneath an aurora, or race above glowing rivers of lava. Hear the wind build as you dive, pull into a sweeping climb, and leave a ribbon of contrails across the sky.

Open your browser and take flight. No installation or account required.

## Four ways to find your flow

**Free Roam — follow your curiosity.** An infinite open world and the freedom to turn in any direction. Follow a coastline, climb above the mountains, or circle back to a favourite lake. Follow optional golden rings as they appear ahead, build a streak for growing coin rewards, reach distance and altitude milestones, or keep going until the landscape changes around you. Enable loop-the-loop in Settings for a little extra freedom. No route to follow. Nowhere you have to be.

**Free Race — chase the light.** Race through towering gates before they close, using your radar to find the next turn. Gates refill your boost completely and pay 100 coins to begin with. Every ten consecutive clears raises the reward, up to 1,000 coins per gate. The lights pulse orange as time runs out; miss one and your streak resets, but the race keeps going. Deadlines adapt to your glider, so faster folds still have a race on their hands. An endless route through a world that never stops unfolding.

**Freestyle — make the scenery your playground.** Skim lakes, weave through trees, hug a ridge, and thread tight gaps to build a growing combo. Enable loop-the-loop in Settings to add full loops to your tricks. Mix your moves, fly closer, and carry more speed to score higher. Level out and fly clear for three seconds to bank your points and earn coins. Crash before banking and that combo is gone. Find your own line across an infinite, changing landscape.

**Ring runs — chase the perfect flight.** Twenty-six dedicated levels, each with its own atmosphere. Time your throw, trade altitude for speed, and thread glowing rings to grow your multiplier. Seek out vivid bonus rings worth three times the usual reward. Boost through a narrow arch, slip past a stone pillar, and push for a new personal best. Keep the streak alive and the rewards keep climbing. In moonlit **Tunnel Run**, choose between three passages: follow the gold rings to keep your streak, or take the wrong tunnel and start a new one.

Distance in Free Roam. Gates in Free Race. Banked points in Freestyle. A personal best for every level, and always something to come back for.

## A world that keeps unfolding

The landscapes are **procedurally generated as you fly**, stretching the journey beyond a fixed map. Free Roam, Free Race, and Freestyle share thirteen biomes that blend smoothly into one another: emerald coastlines, sunset mesas, frost mountains, amethyst dunes, moonlit wetlands, northern-light snowfields, cherry-blossom terraces, waterfall cliffs, glowing night forests, volcanic ridges, stormy highlands, dense jungle canopy, and lotus-covered wetlands. Terrain, scenery, colour, and light shift together as you cross between them.

## Find your wings

Choose from **thirteen distinct paper planes**, from the familiar Original to more capable gliders with different speed, handling, lift, boost, and coin rewards. Earn coins through ring streaks, race gates, Freestyle combos, and Free Roam milestones, then unlock your next plane or destination. Work towards **The Ultima**, the fastest fold in the fleet, with exceptional boost endurance for endless racing.

Make each glider your own in the **Workshop**: choose its colours, pattern and finish, then leave a trail of mist, amber, aurora or flowing rainbow light. Drag to turn and tilt the live 3D preview, see your contrails flow, and keep your favourite angle while you fine-tune the design. Choose Plain, Confetti or Camo, with matte paper, glossy foil or glowing neon finishes. Reset an individual glider whenever you want a fresh sheet.

New players start with **420 coins**. Every glider and level can be unlocked through play. Glider is free to play — keep flying to earn your next unlock.

## Your sky, on any device

Start on your computer. Pick up on your phone. Come back tomorrow with your progress waiting.

Create a **save code and four-digit PIN**, then enter them once on each device you want to use. Your coins, unlocked gliders and worlds, personal bests, glider designs and shared settings **sync automatically in both directions** when you're online. No email, account registration, or save files to move around.

Guest progress saves on your device automatically, including regular checkpoints during flight. Cloud saving is optional; once linked, it quietly carries your progress between devices when connected. You can also delete your cloud save from the save menu.

Pitch inversion, optional loops and your preferred left/right touch-control layout stay on each device, so your controller and touchscreen can each feel right.

## Ready when you are

- **Desktop:** steer with your mouse, WASD, or arrow keys. Hold Space to charge your throw, release to launch, and hold again to boost.
- **Mobile:** dedicated thumb controls keep steering and boost comfortably separate. Swap their sides in Settings if you prefer steering with your right thumb.
- **Xbox controller:** steer with the left stick, use A to select and fly, and hold RT to charge your throw or boost. Play in the browser or the installed app, with controller prompts throughout the menus.
- **Make it yours:** adjust sensitivity, pitch inversion, sound, music and visual quality. Auto quality adapts to your device; optional **Enhanced graphics** adds soft sunlight shadows, richer lighting and water highlights on more capable hardware.
- **Keep it close:** add Glider to your home screen and play offline once the game is cached. Cloud progress catches up when you're back online.

Every player's distance contributes to the shared total on the homepage, including guests. Watch the rolling kilometre counter climb as the community explores. Fly solo. Add a little more sky to everyone's journey.
