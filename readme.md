**A little paper plane. A beautiful escape.**

Launch into Glider, a free 3D flying game about finding your flow. Dive for speed, climb towards the clouds, and thread glowing rings as the landscape rushes beneath your wings. Every close call begs for one more flight.

- **Seven places to get lost in.** Golden canyons, emerald islands, frozen caverns, violet dunes, floating gardens, moonlit marshes, and an open world of green hills and turquoise lakes.
- **Fly your own way.** Chase rings and beat your personal best, or explore Free Flight in any direction. No rings there—just boost, glide, and sky.
- **Your next flight is a click away.** Play on desktop or mobile, with smooth mouse steering or dedicated thumb controls. Your records stay saved on your device.

Free to play. No account needed.
